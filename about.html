Our Story

Built by two brothers
tired of hand-washing.

Kamado Car Wash started in 2019 as a single automated bay in Randburg and has grown into a two-site operation serving Johannesburg drivers every day of the week.

History

From one bay to two lanes

Founders Nikita and Ausar Kamado opened the first Kamado tunnel wash in Randburg in 2019, after one too many Saturday mornings spent queuing at a hand car wash. They set out to build something faster, more consistent, and kinder to the environment than a bucket-and-sponge crew. A second, larger location followed in Sandton in 2022, and today Kamado Car Wash washes over 400 cars a day across both sites. See what a wash includes on our services page.

Mission & Vision

What drives us

Our Mission

To give our customers in Johannesburg a fast, reliable and water-conscious car wash, without compromising on quality or price.

Our Vision

To become Gauteng's most trusted car wash brand, known as much for our environmental responsibility as for our shine.

Our Values

How we operate

Speed with care

A full wash takes four minutes, but every stage is tuned so speed never means a rushed, patchy clean.

Water responsibility

Our recycling system reclaims the majority of rinse water used in every wash cycle.

Fair, transparent pricing

What you see on the kiosk screen is what you pay — no add-on surprises at the till.

Community first

We sponsor two local school fundraising car washes a year and hire from the neighbourhoods we serve.

Who We Serve

Built for busy drivers

Our customers are commuters, families and small delivery fleets across Sandton and Randburg who want a consistently clean car without spending a Saturday morning on it — from daily commuters swinging past on the way to work, to fleet managers who need several vehicles washed on a schedule.
