How it works

One lane, five processes, zero exhaustion.

Every package goes through the same five stages; the packages differ in what each stage adds. Compare the stages on our services and pricing page.

01

Drive In

Come towards the sensor, pick your desired package by the kiosk, and continue forward to the guide rail.

02

Pre-Soak

A foam bath is done to loosen up the dust, pollen and classic road grime before a single brush is in contact with the car's paint.

03

Wash & Scrub

A soft touch cloth and rotating wheel brushes are used to remove dirt from the body, rims and lower panels of the vehicle.

04

Rinse

High pressure, recycled water jets rinse off all traces of soap, preparing for the finish.

05

Dry & Shine

Lastly, turbo air dryers and a spray wax are used to leave your car dirt free and shiny in no time.
